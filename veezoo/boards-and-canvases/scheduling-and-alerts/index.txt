15:T1579,<p>Instead of opening Veezoo to check your numbers, you can have Veezoo come to you: <strong>Boards</strong> and <a href="/veezoo/agents"><strong>Agents</strong></a> can run on a schedule and deliver their results to Email, Slack or Microsoft Teams. With free-text send conditions, a schedule becomes an <strong>alert</strong> that only notifies you when something is worth your attention.</p>
<p><img src="/img/generated/schedule-dialog.png" alt="The Create Schedule dialog with frequency, time, timezone, alert instructions and delivery channel"></p>
<h2 id="scheduling-a-board">Scheduling a board</h2>
<ol>
<li>Open the board and click the <strong>Schedule</strong> button in the board footer.</li>
<li>Configure when it should run and where it should be delivered (see below).</li>
<li>Click <strong>Create Schedule</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>On every run, Veezoo refreshes the board's widgets, summarizes the results and sends them to the configured channel. You can change a schedule later via <strong>Edit Schedule</strong> / <strong>Update Schedule</strong>, or remove it with <strong>Delete Schedule</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="scheduling-an-agent">Scheduling an agent</h2>
<p>Agents - reusable analyses defined by instructions - can be scheduled the same way. In the agent dialog, choose <strong>Add Schedule</strong> (or <strong>Edit &#x26; Schedule Agent</strong>) and configure timing and delivery. On each run, the agent executes its instructions from scratch and sends its findings to the chosen channel.</p>
<aside class="docs-aside docs-aside--note" data-title="Note"><p class="docs-aside__title">Note</p><p>Scheduled agents run in read-only mode: they can query data and write their report, but cannot modify the Knowledge Graph or write VKL.</p></aside>
<h2 id="schedule-configuration">Schedule configuration</h2>
<p>Schedules are defined as a simple sentence - "Run daily at 09:00" - with these options:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Frequency</strong>:
<ul>
<li><strong>Daily</strong> - every day, or only on weekdays (Mon-Fri).</li>
<li><strong>Weekly</strong> - on a specific day of the week.</li>
<li><strong>Monthly</strong> - on a specific day of the month.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Times of day</strong>: one or more times per day. Use the plus button to add additional times - for example a morning and an end-of-day delivery.</li>
<li><strong>Timezone</strong>: every schedule has an explicit timezone, so "9:00" means 9:00 in the timezone you pick, independent of server locations and daylight saving time. One click sets it to your own timezone.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="delivery-channels">Delivery channels</h2>
<p>Under <strong>Deliver To</strong>, choose where the results should go:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Email</strong> - sent to your email address.</li>
<li><strong>Slack</strong> - as a direct message to you, or posted to a channel. If Slack is not connected yet, use <strong>+ Connect Slack</strong>. See <a href="/veezoo/integrations/slack">Slack Integration</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Microsoft Teams</strong> - as a direct message, or posted to a channel of a team you select. See <a href="/veezoo/integrations/microsoft-teams">Microsoft Teams Integration</a>.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="run-now">Run Now</h2>
<p>Use <strong>Run Now</strong> to trigger a schedule immediately. This is the easiest way to test that the delivery channel works and that the output looks the way you want, without waiting for the next scheduled run.</p>
<h2 id="turning-a-schedule-into-an-alert">Turning a schedule into an alert</h2>
<p>Every schedule has an optional free-text instructions field - <strong>Instructions</strong> for boards, <strong>Send Conditions &#x26; Format</strong> for agents. Whatever you write there is interpreted by Veezoo on each run, which lets you turn a plain delivery into a smart alert:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Send conditions</strong> - only deliver when something is noteworthy:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>"Only send if you detect an anomaly in any of the widgets."</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<p>"Only send if revenue exceeds $10,000."</p>
</blockquote>
<p>If the condition is not met, nothing is sent - no noise in your inbox or channel.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Follow-up investigation</strong> - tell Veezoo what to dig into before reporting:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>"If new signups dropped more than 10% week-over-week, investigate further by breaking signups down by country and acquisition channel."</p>
</blockquote>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Formatting</strong> - control how results are presented:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>"Be concise, lead with the three most important changes, and use emojis in headers."</p>
</blockquote>
</li>
</ul>
<p>You can combine all three in one instruction, for example:</p>
<pre><code># Condition to Send
Only send if you detect an anomaly, like a sudden drop or spike in any KPI.

# Additional Investigation
If revenue is down versus last week, break it down by region and product line.

# Formatting
Be concise and use emojis in headers.
</code></pre>
<p>Leave the field empty to always deliver with default formatting.</p>
